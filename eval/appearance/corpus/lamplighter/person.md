# The Lamplighter

A fictional figure invented for this fixture — one of the recurring archetypes a dream project reaches for when it needs someone who tends something at the edge of a scene.

## Archetype

keeper of small lights

## Voice

few words, spoken late; answers a question with the part of it that matters

## Appearance

- build: narrow, stooped from years of reaching upward
- hair: cropped close, gone entirely grey at the temples
- face: long, deeply lined around the eyes from squinting at flame
- dress: a canvas coat past the knee, oil-stained at the cuffs
- distinguishing marks: a burn scar across the back of the right hand

Carries the pole always, even when there is nothing to light.
