# Murakami World

The liminal, melancholy space several of Murakami's narrators pass through — half memory, half elsewhere, recurring across separate books without ever being quite the same place twice.

## Locus Type

narrative-place

## Genius Loci

Quiet, rain-damp, given to long silences and wells that go further down than they should. Time moves strangely here; a conversation can outlast the night it started in, and a place from one book can turn up, unannounced, in the margins of another.
